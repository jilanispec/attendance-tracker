Attendance entry by date

Saturday and Sunday are holidays by default. If a weekday is declared a holiday, mark it as a holiday and mark the Saturday that replaces it as a working day.

A declared holiday has zero conducted sessions and is excluded from the attendance denominator. A normal day not entered by 6 PM is marked absent for all configured sessions when you next open the site. Weekends are holidays unless you explicitly set a Saturday as working.

Calendar & semester settings

When a weekday holiday is replaced by a working Saturday, mark the weekday as a holiday and the Saturday as “Saturday working day (WD timetable)”. The app records attendance per date and does not assume that the weekday's subject timetable automatically transfers; select the actual subjects for the Saturday's sessions.

Data is stored in this browser only; it does not sync automatically across devices. Export backups regularly. This is a personal tracker, not an official college attendance record.
